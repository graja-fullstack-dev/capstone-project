# SOP-005 Product Quality Complaint Triage

Product quality complaints describe a defect or concern with the product itself, such as damaged packaging, discoloration, foreign particles, broken tablets, leaking containers, missing leaflets or labelling errors.

Triage steps: record the product, lot number and a precise description of the defect; note whether a sample is available for return; assign the status New. A reviewer moves the report to In Review when investigation starts. If the complaint also mentions a health event, a separate Suspected Adverse Event report must be created and cross-referenced by reference identifier.

Complaint trends are reviewed by searching for the lot number and product name across saved reports.
