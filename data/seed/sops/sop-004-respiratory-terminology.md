# SOP-004 Recording Respiratory Terms in Event Descriptions

Purpose: help operators record reporter wording consistently. This SOP is about documentation only and gives no clinical guidance.

Reporters often use everyday language. Record the reporter's words verbatim and, where helpful, add the standard term in the event description. Examples of everyday wording and the matching standard term:
- difficulty breathing, short of breath, breathless: dyspnea
- throat tightness, swelling of the face or lips with breathing trouble: may be described using the term anaphylaxis if the reporter uses that word
- wheezing: wheeze

Do not interpret severity, do not decide whether the event is related to the product, and do not tell the reporter what to do medically. If the reporter asks for medical advice, advise them to contact a healthcare professional and escalate the report internally.
