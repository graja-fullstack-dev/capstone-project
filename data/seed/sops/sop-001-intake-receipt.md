# SOP-001 Receipt and Logging of Safety Intake Reports

Purpose: describe how intake operators log product quality complaints and suspected adverse event reports received by email or telephone. All content in this training prototype is fictional.

1. Every report received by email or telephone must be logged on the same working day.
2. Select the report category: Suspected Adverse Event or Product Quality Complaint.
3. Record product name, batch/lot number, date received, reporter type and a detailed description in the reporter's own words.
4. The system generates a unique reference identifier (REF-YYYY-NNNN) when the report is saved. Quote this reference in all later communication.
5. Do not record patient names, initials, dates of birth, contact details or medical history. Use fictional, de-identified wording only.
6. Do not assess whether the product caused an event and do not advise the reporter on treatment. Escalate such questions to the qualified safety team.
