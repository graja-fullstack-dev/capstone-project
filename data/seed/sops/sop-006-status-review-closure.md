# SOP-006 Status Lifecycle, Review and Closure

Reports move through three statuses in order: New, In Review, Closed. Skipping a status or moving backwards is not permitted. Closed is final.

Moving a report from New to In Review is recorded immediately in the audit trail.

Closing a report (In Review to Closed) requires human compliance approval. When closure is requested the report is frozen: no further status change is possible until a human reviewer records an explicit approval or rejection with their reviewer code (format REV-NN). The reviewer must be a different person from the requester. An automated assistant may request closure but may never approve it. Rejection returns the report to its In Review state for further work.
